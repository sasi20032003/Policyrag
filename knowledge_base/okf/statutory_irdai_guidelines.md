# IRDAI Health Insurance Standardization Norms

## 1. Non-Contestable Moratorium Clause (5-Year Rule)
Under the 2024–2026 master regulations, after **5 continuous years of coverage (60 months)**, an insurer cannot reject any health insurance claim on grounds of non-disclosure or misrepresentation of medical facts, unless deliberate bad-faith fraud is proven in a civil court.

## 2. 30-Day Initial Waiting Period
All illnesses (except those arising directly out of accidental injury) are excluded during the initial 30 days from policy inception. Accidental hospitalizations are admissible from Day 1.
